Elon Musk Reveals The Tesla Roadster

 Sources: https://www.tesla.com/roadster

Elon Musk, owner of SpaceX and Tesla, after years of teasing, revealed the Tesla Roadster: a car that can go 0 - 60 in less than a second, can drive upside down, and most importantly, it can fly.

It uses cold gas thrusters to bring it into the air, and it has suction fans on the bottom to stick to surfaces so it can go upside down.
